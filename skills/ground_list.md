## Ground List

- **Action Keyword:** ground_list
- **Says:** list the folder, what is in the folder, review the logs, check the logs, read the logs, look at the logs, the logs, can you list it, list it, the full repo, list the repo, list the full repo, review the repo, what files, list the files, list the dir, list the directory, list dir, in this repo, in the repo, whats in the repo, what is in this repo, list the ground, whats in the ground
- **Description:** Lists what is actually in the ground (the Research folder) right now — the total listed, then top-level folders with counts, and files. Quote the total; never count the lines yourself. Live from disk, read-only. Use for "what is in this repo", "list the ground", "what files are here".

- **Path Args:** content -> ground
