# Skill: List the Rack
- **Action Keyword:** rack_list
- **Says:** what is on the rack, models on the rack, what models, which models, list models, the rack, whats on the card, whats loaded, what is loaded, whats in vram, is opencode running, whats warm, what is available
- **Description:** Reports every model installed on this machine, which of them are currently loaded in VRAM, their sizes, and which are declared by this ground versus held by another client. Read-only. Use it to answer what models are available or what is taking up the card.
- **Parameters Needed:** None
